turboreld is starting as {{ include "turborel.fullname" . }}-0 in {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

It listens in the cluster at
  {{ include "turborel.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Connect with the command-line client from your machine:

  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "turborel.fullname" . }} 7422:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  turborel --host 127.0.0.1 --port 7422

Users are managed with `turboreld user` on a database no server holds;
the manual says how (doc/operator/05-users.md in the turborel repository).
